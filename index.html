Saraj Square

Sarajevo Bosnia and Herzegovina

An experimental typeface drawn from Sarajevo's architecture.

Saraj Square was developed for the exhibition "Eksperimentala" in Sarajevo. The project reimagines how we read type in relation to city space, taking its letterforms directly from the architectural elements that shape the city's skyline and streets. Designed in collaboration with Ibro Karagić.

Murakami Series

Sarajevo Book Covers

A concept redesign of Haruki Murakami novels, inspired by woodblock prints.

A concept redesign of three Murakami books: Kafka on the Shore, The Elephant Vanishes, and Men Without Women. Inspired by Japanese woodblock prints and a clean visual aesthetic, the covers began as analogue prints that were then digitalized and developed into the final designs. Each of the prints serves as an element of the patterns created for the backing of the books.

Pecha Kucha Sarajevo

Sarajevo Bosnia and Herzegovina

A visual identity for a fast-paced event for young, upcoming artists.

This identity was proposed for Pecha Kucha Sarajevo 2025. The concept comes from two things: the very short presentation time each artist gets, and their "young flame" status in the art world. Matches became the central image, a small flame that burns quickly and brightly. The visuals use bold, bright colors and a matchbox character derived from the original Pecha Kucha logo, giving the event an energetic, youthful feel.

HOME

Lisbon Portugal

A space that meets your exact needs.

HOME is an interactive space designed for slowing down and looking inward. Shaped around one person, the room adjusts its light, temperature and atmosphere to what they need in the moment, creating a calm that is entirely theirs. The design focus was the interaction itself: how the room speaks, listens and changes to hold that space.
